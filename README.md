# Alice Cultivation Station

**Enchanted botanical analysis — [alice-botanyca.com](https://alice-botanyca.com)**

A modular, 3D-printable growing station that raises and lowers a plant
net and grow light smoothly at **four independent corners**. Designed for
greenhouses, grow tents, and home cultivation — print it, build it, and
give your plants gentle, controlled movement.

> **Status:** Work in progress. Files are being finalized and added
> gradually. ⭐ Star / watch the repo to follow along.

---

## ✨ What it does

- **4-point lifting** — each corner has its own motorized column
  (NEMA 17 stepper + lead screw) so the plant net and light can rise and
  lower evenly, or corner-by-corner.
- **Gentle movement modes** — controlled from the companion app:
  - **Net Control** — raise/lower the net or the top light by precise steps.
  - **Wave** — a soft, wave-like motion across the net.
  - **Give Love** — a tiny cyclic micro-movement to gently refresh the
    plant support area.
- **Clean, hidden build** — cables are routed inside the frame for a tidy
  finished look.
- **Modular** — corners are independent units, so the station scales to
  different bed sizes.

## 🖨️ Printing (short version)

1. Open the `.3mf` files (or the STLs once available) in your slicer
   (PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, Cura, etc.).
2. Print the corner parts — you will need **4 corners** for one station.
3. Hardware (motors, lead screws, inserts, fasteners) — a full parts list
   will be published here as the design is finalized.
