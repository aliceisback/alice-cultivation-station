Help & Instructions

It's very easy to control the lifters.

Choose how many centimeters you want to move the system, then select Up or Down. You can move all four lifters together, or adjust each corner separately when needed.

The app shows the current position of the lifters and keeps movement within safe limits.

Use Emergency Stop at any time to stop all movement immediately.
